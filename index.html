Scientific compute infrastructure

DIU OS is infrastructure for building, running, and sealing scientific modules. It is not EdTech and not a DeSci protocol. The product is diu-os.com. This domain is the public showcase.

What is public

  • manifesto — epistemic charter (Apache-2.0). Living text: MANIFESTO.md. PDF v1.0.0 (Dec 2025) is a prior-art snapshot.
  • diu-contracts — Arbitrum Stylus contracts on Sepolia testnet (MIT). Not a live mirror of the commercial workspace.

What is not published here

  • Platform source (API, frontend, engines) lives in a private commercial repository.
  • Developer docs, MCP servers, and a public SDK are not shipped. diu-os.dev is a placeholder.
  • No mainnet deployment. No NFT certificates. No VR/AR labs.
